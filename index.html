Big Data Projects on Wheat

Wheat (Triticum aestivum) is an important crop whose reference genome offers a valuable resource for understanding wheat genetic structure, improving agronomic traits, and developing new cultivars.
I was tasked to develop a processing and analytical workflow to screen the proteome (all the proteins) of thousands of cultivars.

I first developped a fast, accurate and reproducible method which I then applied to 4,087 wheat grain samples.
I produced, managed and analysed close to 8,000 files representing >1.2 Gb of data.

I employed sophisticated methods of statistical analyses, bioinformatics and data mining to identify protein biomarkers of late-maturity alpha-amylase (LMA) and generate meaningful biological insights.
LMA-affected grains activated their primary metabolisms such as glycolysis and gluconeogenesis; TCA cycle, along with DNA- and RNA- binding mechanisms; and protein translation.
This logically transitioned to protein folding activities driven by chaperones and protein disulfide isomerase , as well as protein assembly via dimerisation and complexing.
The secondary metabolism was also mobilized with the upregulation of phytohormones and chemical and defence responses. LMA further invoked cellular structures, including ribosomes, microtubules, and chromatin.
LMA expression greatly impacted grain storage proteins, as well as starch and other carbohydrates, with the upregulation of alpha-gliadins and starch metabolism, whereas LMW glutenin, stachyose , sucrose , UDP-galactose , and UDP-glucose were downregulated.

Proteins were then mapped onto the wheat genome (IWGSC RefSeq v2.1) to provide experimental support for high-confidence (HC) gene models, strengthen the evidence for low-confidence (LC) gene models, and identify proteogenomic signals relevant to genome-annotation refinement.
Two complementary peptide-to-genome mapping strategies were used:
1/ tBLASTn-based approach, in which peptide sequences were aligned directly against the wheat genome to detect sequence-level matches;
2/ GFF3 annotation-guided pipeline, in which peptide coordinates were reconstructed programmatically from existing gene, transcript, CDS, and protein annotations.
The second strategy enabled more precise genome-context mapping, including exon-aware placement of peptides, distinction between within-exon and intron-spanning evidence, validation against translated genomic sequence, and export of browser-ready tracks for Apollo/JBrowse visualisation. This strategy validated 98% of gene models.

Visual Automation

I am a keen photgrapher and have taken a plethora of digital pictures for the past 3 decades. In May 2025, I started to share them on social media.
My chanels @60_seconds_of_calm showcases my travels, garden, and travels.
I am using a Python program to automate the creation of an aesthetic photo montage using Python and FFmpeg.
It processes a folder of images, applies fade transitions, and enriches the final video with ambient sounds and a piano music.
There are 15 themes (castle, church, national parks, cities, villages, garden, art, whimsical etc...) which are processed in batch.
A stylised "door opening & closing" animation that I creayed overlays the video to simulate stepping outside for a 1 min break.
Each video contains 20 images, which are being processed as described below.
You can view those videos on YouTube and TikTok.

Technologies Used
  • Python (OpenCV, Pillow)
  • FFmpeg (Video compression, audio mixing, overlay effects)
  • Command-line automation using subprocess

Workflow Steps
  1. Load Images: Count all JPG/PNG files in a folder.
  2. Resize & Prepare: Each image is padded and resized to fit a 1920×1080 frame while preserving its aspect ratio.
  3. Montage Creation: A video is generated with each image shown for 2 seconds, and smooth crossfades between them using OpenCV.
  4. Video Compression: The montage is compressed with FFmpeg using the H.264 codec (CRF=28) to reduce file size.
  5. Ambient Sound: A high-quality nature soundscape is layered under the video with fade-in and fade-out effects.
  6. Background Music: A piano loop is blended with the ambient sound using precise volume and fading controls.
  7. Door Animation Overlay: A stylised "door opening" appears at the beginning, and a "door closing" fades in at the end, overlayed on the video.
  8. Final Output: A beautifully compressed MP4 (1080p, H.264) video montage, complete with ambient atmosphere and musical mood.
